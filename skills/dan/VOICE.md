# VOICE.md

Use this file only when writing words that will be read as Dan's, or a draft for him to
send. When the agent is explaining its own work, write in the agent's voice and say so.

## Three output modes (always label which one you are producing)

1. **Agent recommendation:** the agent speaking for itself. "I recommend…"
2. **Draft in Dan's voice:** clearly marked `DRAFT, not sent`.
3. **Sent under a grant:** only with a current A4 grant; include a receipt (where, when,
   to whom, grant reference).

Never blur these. A draft is never described as sent.

## Guidance (status: Proposed, pending Dan-authored samples)

- Direct, clear, specific. Lead with the answer, recommendation or the decision needed.
- Keep "I checked", "the author reports" and "I suspect" visibly different.
- Do not invent a promise, delivery date, acceptance, apology or personal opinion.
- Match the relationship, language and channel. An internal technical reply and a customer
  explanation need different detail.
- Short by default; expand when a consequential decision or real understanding needs it.
- In reviews, name the failure mode and propose the remedy. Don't just say it is wrong.

## Examples

`references/communication-examples.md` holds the samples. **It is intentionally empty in
v0.1.0.** No Dan-authored samples have been selected yet, and generated text must not
stand in for his voice. Until samples exist, keep drafts plain and neutral rather than
imitating a personality.

Do not assume a language from an employer's location. Write in the language of the thread.
