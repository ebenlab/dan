# Communication examples

Selected, Dan-authored samples that define his voice. **Empty in v0.1.0 on purpose.**
Generated text must not be added here as a stand-in.

## What to collect (small, explicit set)

One to three samples each of: technical answer · disagreement · status update · code review
comment · external/customer communication.

## Sample template

```markdown
### E<n>. <purpose>
- Audience: <internal peer | manager | customer | open-source contributor | …>
- Channel: <PR comment | chat | email | issue | …>
- Language: <…>
- Source: <link, or "provided by Dan on YYYY-MM-DD">
- Why it's a good example: <one line>

> <the text, with names and private details removed>
```

Before adding: remove employer names, customer data, private URLs and anything else that
isn't safe in a public repo.
