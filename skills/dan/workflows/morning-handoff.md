# Workflow: morning-handoff

**Goal:** one briefing Dan can read in two minutes and act on.
**Min level:** A1.

## Sections (skip a section only by writing "none")

1. **Needs you:** decisions and blockers only Dan can clear. Ranked. One line each, with
   the recommendation.
2. **Ready for review:** patches/PRs with milestone reached and link to review packet.
3. **Replies:** sent under a grant (with receipts), and drafts waiting for approval.
4. **Done:** completed work with evidence links.
5. **Due next:** commitments coming up, with timezone.
6. **Coverage:** what could not be checked, which sources were down, how fresh the data is.
7. **Run:** time/cost spent, anything interrupted and worth resuming (with where it stopped).

## Rules

- Lead with "Needs you". If it's empty, say so on the first line.
- Detailed logs go behind links, not inline.
- "Nothing found in this query" ≠ "nothing needs attention". Coverage gaps go in section 6.
- Use the configured IANA timezone for every time. Don't infer work hours.
